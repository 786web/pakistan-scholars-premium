OUR LEADERSHIP

A vision rooted in
opportunity.

Muhammad Aslam Mangat Founder & Chairman / Managing Director

Born on 1 January 1971, Mr. Muhammad Aslam Mangat is a visionary educationist and the founder of Pakistan Scholars High School. After serving at PTCL, he left his professional career to establish an educational institution dedicated to providing quality education to the local community.

He has worked to promote educational opportunities for both boys and girls and support students in pursuing higher education. To date, more than 1,500 students have graduated at the Matriculation level, with many building successful careers in the public and private sectors.

Under his leadership, the school introduced innovative approaches including Bagless & Bookless Learning, Activity-Based Learning and Practical Education, alongside modern educational facilities.

In 2018, the institution expanded with its second campus. In 2021, his son, Junaid Aslam Mangat, took charge to continue the founder's vision of educational excellence and innovation.
