A VISION FOR EDUCATION · SINCE 2004
OUR LEADERSHIP

A vision rooted in
opportunity.

Muhammad Aslam Mangat Founder & Chairman

Born on 1 January 1971, Mr. Muhammad Aslam Mangat is a visionary educationist and the founder of Pakistan Scholars Educational System M.B. DIN. After serving at PTCL, he left his professional career to establish an educational institution dedicated to providing quality education to the local community.

He has worked to promote educational opportunities for both boys and girls and support students in pursuing higher education. To date, more than 1,500 students have graduated at the Matriculation level, with many building successful careers in the public and private sectors.

Under his leadership, the school introduced innovative approaches including Bagless & Bookless Learning, Activity-Based Learning and Practical Education, alongside modern educational facilities.

In 2018, the institution expanded with its second campus. In 2021, his son, Junaid Aslam Mangat, took charge to continue the founder's vision of educational excellence and innovation.

HIS VISION
“To empower future generations through quality education, character building, and innovative learning.”
BHOA HASSAN CAMPUS · LEADERSHIP
CAMPUS LEADERSHIP · BHOA HASSAN

Leading with
purpose and progress.

Junaid Aslam Mangat Managing Director · Bhoa Hassan Campus

Born on 15 October 1996, Mr. Junaid Aslam Mangat holds an M.Sc. (Hons.) degree from the University of Agriculture, Faisalabad. After completing his education in 2021, he took charge of Pakistan Scholars Educational System M.B. DIN, Bhoa Hassan Campus, with a vision to promote quality education and educational innovation.

Since joining, he has been actively working to improve academic standards, strengthen management, and introduce modern teaching practices. Under his leadership, the campus continues to progress and develop.

His vision is to provide students with a unique and inspiring learning environment that goes beyond traditional education, focusing on academic excellence, creativity, confidence, and character building.

He is committed to bringing meaningful change to the education sector by nurturing responsible, skilled, and morally strong individuals who can contribute positively to society and the future of Pakistan.

HIS VISION
“To create an educational environment that transforms thinking, builds character, and sets new standards for future generations.”
